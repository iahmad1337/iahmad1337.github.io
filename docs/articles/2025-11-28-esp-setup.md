Once you connect esp32 to computer via usb, it won't be visible in lsusb right
off the bat. What you have to do is to [establish serial
connection](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32/get-started/establish-serial-connection.html)

The port of my devboard is `/dev/ttyUSB0` - it can be determined by
plugging/unplugging the board and tracking the difference.
